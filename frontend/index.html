TorMap: world map of Tor relays since 2007

TorMap shows where the public relays of the Tor network are hosted, on a world map and for any day since October 2007. It is free, open source and has no ads.

What you can explore

  • The whole history: pick any day since October 2007, or follow the number of relays over time on the timeline.
  • Grouping: group relays by family, autonomous system (AS), country or coordinates, or show a density heatmap.
  • Filters: show exit, guard or other relays, require relay flags, or only show the relays with the highest consensus weight.
  • Tor users per country: color countries by the estimated number of Tor users on that day.
  • Relay details: search any relay by nickname or fingerprint and see its descriptor data, AS and confirmed family members.

Data sources

TorMap processes the relay descriptors from the Tor Project's CollecTor archive and the user estimates from Tor Metrics. Each relay's location and autonomous system come from IP geolocation data by DB-IP. New data is added every day.
